About AESCO

AESCO is the wire and cable brand of Ajay Enterprises, an ISO 9001 certified company with 25+ years of industry experience.

Who we are

Established in 2001, Ajay Enterprises is an ISO 9001 certified company and a fast-growing manufacturer of high-quality wires and cables, backed by over 25 years of industry experience.

From this legacy, AESCO was born, a brand serving residential, commercial and industrial applications. Under AESCO, our product range includes PVC Insulated Submersible Flat Cables, Aluminium Cables, Single Core Flexible Cables, Multicore Flexible Cables, Power Cables and Control Cables.

Built on a foundation of trust, quality and expertise, AESCO is committed to delivering reliable cable solutions and powering the nation's progress.

What we work towards

Our vision

To become a trusted and recognised name in the electrical manufacturing industry by delivering innovative, safe and reliable wiring solutions that power progress and support modern infrastructure.

Our mission

To manufacture electrical wires and cables that are safe, durable and dependable in agricultural, residential, commercial and industrial applications.

Homes, businesses, industry and farms

AESCO serves residential, commercial and industrial applications. Our submersible flat cables are also widely used in agriculture.

  • Residential

    Wiring for homes and residential projects, and cables for household water pumps.

    • Single Core Flexible Cables
    • Submersible Flat Cables

  • Commercial

    Wiring for small commercial setups, pump connections in commercial buildings, and cables for commercial and infrastructure projects.

    • Single Core Flexible Cables
    • Power Cables
    • Aluminium Cables

  • Industrial

    Flexible cables for machinery and panels, control cables for switchgear and automation, power cables for motors and plant, and pump cables for continuous operation.

    • Multicore Flexible Cables
    • Control Cables
    • Power Cables
    • Submersible Flat Cables

  • Agriculture

    Submersible flat cables for borewell pumps and irrigation systems.
