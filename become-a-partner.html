What you get as an AESCO dealer

AESCO dealers sell six families of wires and cables, made in compliance with ISI standards, with marketing support and product training from our team.

  1. 01

    Six cable families to sell

    Single core and multicore flexible cables, submersible flat cables, and aluminium, control and power cables.

  2. 02

    Marketing and brand support

    Catalogues, brochures, digital creatives and branding support to help you sell in your area.

  3. 03

    Product training and technical help

    Training on our products and technical support, so you can answer customers with confidence.

  4. 04

    ISI compliant, tested in house

    Products made in compliance with ISI standards and tested in house for safety and durability.
