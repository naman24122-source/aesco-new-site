FR and FRLS wires: what the letters mean

House wire in India is usually sold as FR or FRLS. The letters describe how the insulation behaves in a fire, and they decide where each wire should be used.

  2. FRLS: flame retardant, low smoke

Most house wire in India has a copper conductor insulated with PVC. Plain PVC can catch fire, can carry a flame along the wire, and gives off thick smoke as it burns. FR and FRLS are kinds of PVC insulation made to behave better in a fire.

FR: flame retardant

FR stands for flame retardant. The insulation is made with additives that make it harder to set alight and slow a flame from travelling along the wire. Once the flame that started it is taken away, FR insulation tends to stop burning on its own instead of spreading the fire along the wiring.

FRLS: flame retardant, low smoke

FRLS stands for flame retardant, low smoke. It does what FR insulation does, and it is also made to give off less smoke and less acid gas if it does burn.

The acid gas matters because PVC contains chlorine. When ordinary PVC burns, it releases hydrochloric acid gas, which is harmful to breathe and corrodes metal and electronics nearby. FRLS compounds are made to cut down both the smoke and this gas.

Why low smoke matters

In a building fire, smoke often does more harm than the flames. It fills corridors and stairways, hides the way out and is dangerous to breathe. Wiring runs through every floor of a building, so cables that make less smoke make it easier for people to see their way out.

Where each one is used

FR wire is widely used in homes, shops and small buildings, run inside conduit. FRLS is usually chosen where many people gather, or where getting out takes longer:

  • tall apartment blocks and office buildings
  • hospitals, schools and hotels
  • malls, cinemas and other public buildings
  • basements, tunnels and other closed spaces

Some builders choose FRLS for homes as well. If your project has a written specification, follow it.

What the letters do not mean

  • Not fireproof. In a large fire, FR and FRLS insulation will both still burn. They resist starting and spreading a fire, and FRLS makes less smoke.
  • Not halogen free. FRLS is still PVC, so it still contains chlorine. If a project asks for halogen free cable, that is a different material, usually sold as LSZH (low smoke, zero halogen) or ZHFR.
  • Not a different size. The letters say nothing about how much current the wire carries. You still choose the size by the load and the length of the run.

How to tell them apart

Look at the printing along the insulation. It normally shows the type, such as FR or FRLS, along with the size and the voltage grade. Check that the printing and the packing match what you asked for. If anything is unclear, ask the supplier for the product details before you buy.
