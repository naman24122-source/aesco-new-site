Product overview

Our PVC insulated submersible flat cables have stranded copper conductors with PVC insulation, laid side by side under an outer sheath. The cable resists water, moisture and abrasion, so it works safely in wet and underwater conditions.

They supply submersible motors and pumps in borewells, irrigation systems, farm and household water supply, and industrial pumping installations.

The size table covers 0.5 to 50 sq mm, with the strands, insulation, sheath and overall size for each. The cables are made in compliance with ISI standards and tested in house.
