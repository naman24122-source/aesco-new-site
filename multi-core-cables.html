Product overview

Our multicore flexible cables have several insulated cores in one cable. Each core is made of multi-strand copper, which gives the cable its flexibility and strength, and the insulation is heat resistant.

Use them for wiring, panels and machinery, and wherever a cable has to bend, move or vibrate, or fit into a compact wiring layout. They are used in residential, commercial and industrial work.

We make them in compliance with ISI standards and test them in house for safety, durability and performance.
