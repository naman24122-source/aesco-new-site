Product overview

A power cable is chosen by its conductor, its size, its voltage grade and how it will be laid: on trays, in ducts or direct in the ground.

Power cables carry the main supply into homes, commercial buildings and industrial sites, and feed distribution boards, motors, pumps and heavy equipment.

AESCO power cables are made in compliance with ISI standards and tested in house.
