About AESCO

Established in 2001, Ajay Enterprises is an ISO 9001 certified company and a fast-growing manufacturer of high-quality wires and cables, backed by over 25 years of industry experience.

From this legacy, AESCO was born, a brand serving residential, commercial and industrial applications. Under AESCO, our product range includes PVC Insulated Submersible Flat Cables, Aluminium Cables, Single Core Flexible Cables, Multicore Flexible Cables, Power Cables and Control Cables.

Built on a foundation of trust, quality and expertise, AESCO is committed to delivering reliable cable solutions and powering the nation's progress.

Where AESCO cables go in a building

From the street pole and the meter to the distribution board, the room wiring, the machines and the borewell pump, each run uses a different AESCO cable.

  1. Street pole to meter

    Aluminium Cables

    Brings the supply from the street pole to the meter. Aluminium is light and resists corrosion, which suits a long overhead run.

    View Aluminium Cables

  2. Meter to the boards

    Power Cables

    Carries the main supply from the meter to the distribution board, then up to the first floor board and out to the pump starter.

    View Power Cables

  3. Pump starter to tank switch

    Control Cables

    Links the pump starter to the level switch in the roof tank. Several cores in one sheath carry the small signals that switch and watch the pump.

    View Control Cables

  4. Lights, fans and switches

    Single Core Flexible Cables

    The house wiring in the walls and ceilings, feeding every light, fan and switch. Copper conductors with heat and flame resistant insulation.

    View Single Core Flexible Cables

  5. Appliances and machines

    Multicore Flexible Cables

    Connects the TV, fridge, washing machine, geyser and air conditioner. Multi strand copper stays flexible where a cable bends, moves or vibrates.

    View Multicore Flexible Cables

  6. Down the borewell

    PVC Insulated Submersible Flat Cables

    Runs down the borewell to the submersible pump, where the cable sits in water. It is made to resist water, moisture and abrasion.

    View PVC Insulated Submersible Flat Cables
