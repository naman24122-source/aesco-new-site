Who we are

This website is run by Ajay Enterprises of Udaipur, Rajasthan, which makes wires and cables under the brand AESCO Wires and Cables. In this policy, we, us and our mean Ajay Enterprises.

If you have a question about this policy or about your details, email us at info@aesco.co.in.

What this policy covers

This policy covers the personal details you give us through this website: the enquiry, contact and export forms, the become a partner form, the careers form, and any email or call you start from a link on the site. It does not cover other websites that we link to.

We aim to handle your details in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and the rules made under it.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us:

  • Enquiries: your name, phone number, email, your company name and city or country if you give them, the product and quantity you are asking about, and your message.
  • Partner applications: your name, mobile number, email, business name, city and state, the kind of business you run, your turnover band if you give it, the products you would like to sell and any message.
  • Job applications: your full name, email, phone, street address, industry, years of experience, key skills and the CV you attach.

Please do not send us documents such as Aadhaar, PAN or bank details through the website. We do not need them.

Like most websites, the server that hosts this site may keep basic technical records, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visited. These are used to keep the site secure and working.

Why we use it

  • to reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote
  • to review a partner application and talk to you about it
  • to consider you for a job, now or for a later opening
  • to keep the records the law requires us to keep
  • to protect the website from misuse

We do not sell your details, and we do not rent or trade them to anyone. We will not add you to a marketing list unless you ask us to.

Your consent

Each form asks you to tick a box agreeing that we may use your details for that purpose. You can withdraw that consent at any time by emailing info@aesco.co.in. We will then stop using your details for that purpose, unless the law requires us to keep them. Withdrawing consent does not affect what we did before.

Who we share it with

Your details are seen only by the people at AESCO who handle enquiries, partners or hiring. We may also share them with:

  • the companies that host this website or our email, only so that they can provide those services to us
  • government bodies, courts or the police, where the law requires it

Cookies and outside services

This website does not use advertising cookies. Some pages load fonts or other files from outside services, such as Google Fonts. When that happens, your browser connects to that service, which can see your IP address. If we add analytics or other tracking later, we will update this policy first.

How long we keep it

We keep your details only as long as we need them for the reason you sent them, and then delete them, unless the law requires us to keep them longer. If we have no suitable job when you apply, we may keep your CV for a later opening. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

How we protect it

We take reasonable care to keep your details safe, and only the people who need them can see them. No website or email system is completely secure, so please send only what the form asks for.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal details we hold about you and how we use them
  • correct or update them
  • delete them, once we no longer need them
  • stop using them, by withdrawing your consent

You can also name another person to act for you if you are unable to, and you can raise a complaint with us. To do any of these, email info@aesco.co.in. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can take the matter to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

This website is meant for adults and businesses. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us for you.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our forms or services change. The version on this page is the one that applies.
