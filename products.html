Our products

Flexible copper wires for homes, flat cables for borewell pumps, and aluminium, control and power cables. Six families, made in compliance with ISI standards and tested in house.

Six cable families

Every family has its own page. Open one for the full details, or to ask for a quote.

Sizes 0.75 to 6.0 sq mm

Single Core Flexible Cables

Single core flexible cable with a stranded copper conductor and FR or FRLS insulation, used as house wire for concealed and open wiring in homes and small businesses.

  • 1100 V grade, 0.75 to 6 sq mm
  • FR and FRLS insulation
  • Fine stranded copper conductor
View product Single Core Flexible Cables

Multicore Flexible Cables

Multicore flexible cable with multi-strand copper conductors, for wiring, panels and machinery where the cable bends, moves or vibrates while the equipment is working.

  • Multi-strand copper conductors
  • Handles frequent bending, movement and vibration
  • Heat resistant insulation
View product Multicore Flexible Cables
Sizes 0.5 to 50 sq mm

PVC Insulated Submersible Flat Cables

PVC insulated submersible flat cable with stranded copper conductors, for the submersible pumps used in borewells, irrigation, household water supply and industrial pumping.

  • Sizes from 0.5 to 50 sq mm
  • Stranded copper conductors, PVC insulated
  • Resists water, moisture and abrasion
View product PVC Insulated Submersible Flat Cables
Sizes 1.5 to 50 sq mm

Aluminium Cables

Aluminium cable for overhead and underground power distribution, lightweight and corrosion resistant, and suited to long runs in industrial, commercial and infrastructure projects.

  • Light to handle and install
  • Corrosion resistant, for long service life
  • For overhead and underground runs
View product Aluminium Cables

Control Cables

Multicore control cable for the control and signal circuits between panels, switchgear, instruments and machinery, wherever equipment is switched, monitored or interlocked.

  • Several insulated cores under one sheath
  • Many control and signal circuits in one run
  • For panels, switchgear, motor control and automation
View product Control Cables

Power Cables

Power cables carry the main supply to buildings, distribution boards, motors, pumps and plant, and are sized to the load and the length of the run.

  • Main supply to buildings, motors, pumps and plant
  • Chosen by conductor, size and voltage grade
  • Laid on trays, in ducts or direct in the ground
